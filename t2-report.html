ExBank's Risk team needs a weekly escalation trends report with SLA breach flags. Today, they'd file a ticket and wait 6 weeks. With Maia, they describe what they need in plain English and it's built in minutes.
WHAT

What Are We Demonstrating?

A live demonstration of Maia — Matillion's AI assistant — building a production-quality transformation pipeline from a natural language request. The Risk team analyst describes what they need, Maia translates it into visual, low-code components, and the pipeline runs against live Snowflake data.

🗣️

Natural Language → Pipeline

The Risk analyst types a plain English request. Maia builds a full transformation pipeline — joins, filters, calculations, aggregations — without a single line of code.

🧮

Real Business Logic

The Calculator component computes SLA breach flags — comparing actual resolution time against the allowed SLA. Business logic generated from natural language.

✅

Validated Before Execution

Every step is validated and data can be sampled before running. No surprises, no broken pipelines hitting production.

🔒

Governed from Day One

Every pipeline Maia builds is version-controlled in Git, visible on the canvas, and reviewable by the central team. Self-service with guardrails.

WHAT

The Pipeline: Weekly Escalation Trends

1

Load Source Data

Read from three banking tables: operations metrics, business unit dimension, and case type dimension

Table Input × 3
2

Join All Sources

Inner join operations with business units (on UNIT_ID) and case types (on CASE_TYPE_ID) to enrich each record with division, region, severity, and SLA data

Join
3

Filter Escalated Cases

Keep only cases where REQUIRES_ESCALATION = 'Yes' — these are the cases regulators and compliance teams monitor

Filter
4

Flag SLA Breaches ⭐

Compute two new columns: SLA_BREACHED (1 if resolution exceeded SLA, 0 otherwise) and HOURS_OVER_SLA (the difference). This is real business logic from plain English.

CASE WHEN "AVG_RESOLUTION_HOURS" > "SLA_HOURS" THEN 1 ELSE 0 END
Calculator
5

Aggregate Weekly Trends

Group by Division, Region, and Report Week. Sum escalations and SLA breaches, average resolution time, count total records.

Aggregate
6

Rename to Business-Friendly Names

TOTAL_ESCALATIONS, AVG_RESOLUTION_HOURS, SLA_BREACHES, TOTAL_RECORDS — clean names for the dashboard

Rename
7

Write to Target Table

Results land in EXBANK_ESCALATION_TRENDS — 260 rows of weekly trend data ready for the Risk team's dashboard

Rewrite Table
