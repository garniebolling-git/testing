WHAT

The Four Pillars in Detail

🧠 Pillar 1: Context Engine

Matillion's Context Engine captures institutional knowledge alongside pipelines using knowledge graphs. Pipelines, tables, business definitions, and team context are all connected — so when someone new picks up a pipeline, they understand not just what it does, but why.

"If that DataStage engineer had built in Matillion, the knowledge would still be here — in the context graph, not in someone's head."

Navigate to Context Engine in the UI

Show how pipelines, tables, and business context connect in the knowledge graph

🔍 Pillar 2: Data Lineage

Automatic, column-level lineage generated from pipeline runs. No manual documentation required. Trace any output column back through every transformation to its source table.

How to navigate (click by click):
  1. Click "Activity" in the left navigation sidebar
  2. Click "Lineage" under the Activity section
  3. Search for EXBANK_ESCALATION_TRENDS
  4. Click on it — the lineage graph shows sources → transformation → target
  5. Click a dataset box to see column names & data types
  6. Click the transformation icon to see pipeline name, who ran it, when
"Your auditor asks 'Where does the SLA breach number come from?' One click. Source to target, column by column. That's BCBS 239 compliance."

💥 Pillar 3: Impact Analysis

Lineage traces backward: "Where did this come from?" Impact analysis traces forward: "What breaks if I change this?"

EXBANK_OPERATIONS feeds into both Topic 1 (Daily Summary) and Topic 2 (Escalation Trends). Before a DBA renames a column, Impact Stream shows both pipelines as affected.

"You have 200 legacy jobs to modernize. Every time you change a source table, Impact Stream shows you exactly which pipelines depend on it. No blind changes. No Monday morning surprises."

Concrete guarantees for the board and the CISO — not marketing language.

Encryption At rest and in transit. Full stop.
GDPR Data residency controls, right to erasure, the full GDPR framework.
